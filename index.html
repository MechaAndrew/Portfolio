Recent Work

Intergalactic Recovery Service

⯈ ⯈

3D co-op Action Adventure Platformer.


2 Players will take the roles of the IRS Astronauts to retrieve the stolen packages from the "Malians" using their unique tools and teamwork. 


I worked as a 3D Modeler, animator, level designer, and UI designer for this student project.


ShipIt

⯈ ⯈

2.5D Horror Dating Simulator


Aboard a cruise ship, you romanticize the passengers through dialogue and minigames. However, as you try to swoon over the other cruise guests, they mysteriously start to be killed off... 


I worked as a 3D Modeler, texture, and sprite animator for this student project. 


Persistence

Horror Adventure


Trapped in an infinite hallway, you must seek pieces of an ouroboros to try and escape. However with each piece obtained, the rooms become more and more distorted into something else...


I worked as a 3D Modeler for this student project
