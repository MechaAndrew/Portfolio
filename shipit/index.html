Context

⯈ ⯈

This was my send to final project at UC Santa Cruz where I would work with a team of 10 to create a "game slice" using Unreal Engine 5.


Over a 3 month span, this experience would prove to be a big learning experience not just with 3D modeling and texturing, but with proper team coordination.


Work and Experience

⯈ ⯈

I worked as a 3D Modeler, texturer, and sprite animator


We set a large goal for ourselves with this "game slice" as we devised to make a dating simulator with a horror twist. However, with poor communication, we were unable to achieve a demonstrable amount of the horror we envisioned. 


I take this as a great learning experience, as projects going forward, I emphasize communication and consistent meetings..
